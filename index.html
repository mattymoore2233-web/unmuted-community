This is a safe space

Unmuted is peer support — not a crisis service or professional therapy. Moderators are present and the community guidelines are clear.

If you are in immediate danger or having thoughts of harming yourself, please contact emergency services or a crisis line right away.

Our story

The Story Behind Unmuted

Unmuted was founded from a simple but powerful idea by Matthew Moore, who came up with the vision at just 14 years old.

After experiencing his own struggles with mental health, Matthew understood how difficult it can be to speak openly about what you’re going through — especially when you feel pressure to stay strong or worry about being judged. He wanted to create something that could help change that.

Unmuted is a community built to remind men and boys that they don’t have to face everything alone, that opening up is a sign of courage, and that their feelings matter.

The anonymous option was created to give people the freedom to share their thoughts and experiences without feeling exposed or judged, helping them take that first step towards speaking out.

Whether someone needs to get something off their chest, share advice, support another person, or simply know that others understand, Unmuted aims to provide a space where every voice matters.

The mission is to break the stigma surrounding men’s mental health, challenge outdated expectations about masculinity, and build a future where no one feels they have to suffer in silence.

Your voice matters. Your story matters. You are never weak for speaking up.

It’s time to be Unmuted.
